# The earlier BTC pilot

In the 183 sessions from April through September 2026, plain 20-session momentum returned **+9.72%** after costs and the same rule with a 64-return spectral-entropy gate returned **+1.36%**. The difference using the unrounded returns was **−8.37 percentage points**. The gate spent less time in BTC and missed profitable stretches. Buy and hold earned **+22.50%** and cash earned **0%**. [Original metrics](../../reports/btc-momentum-pilot/metrics.json) · [Independent accounting audit](../../reports/btc-pilot-audit/audit.json)

That pilot trained in April–December 2025 and carried its January–March 2026 validation positions into evaluation. Q2 2025 had already been examined. It used the next UTC open as an idealized execution proxy. Its earlier failed period-order check and subsequent successful audit are preserved as separate records. [Frozen pilot protocol](../../experiments/btc-momentum-pilot/protocol.json) · [First-attempt checks](../../reports/btc-momentum-pilot-attempt-1/verification.json)

The five-year study uses 2017–2019 training, later minute-level execution proxies and a passive BTC/cash benchmark. Its results were retrospective and were designed with knowledge of the pilot. The two studies have separate protocols and denominators. The public beginner command reproduces the five-year study. [Five-year example](../../examples/btc-study/README.md)
