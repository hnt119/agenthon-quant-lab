# BTC/USD observation source

The forward study uses Coin Metrics Community reference rates as a paper valuation and fill proxy. The historical archive measured trade-bearing Bitstamp candles. A multi-market reference fixing incorporates different venues and construction and has no executable size, bid/ask spread, or promise of a fill. The venue and pricing-convention change limits direct comparison with the historical findings.

## Exact observations

- Endpoint: HTTPS GET on `community-api.coinmetrics.io/v4/timeseries/asset-metrics`. Asset `btc`, currency USD, frequency `1d` or `1m`. No credentials, account, order endpoint, automatic retries, redirects or alternative venue.
- The most recent close is the daily `ReferenceRateUSD` with event time exactly the midnight ending the completed UTC day. For a 20-session signal the preceding 20 daily `PriceUSD` rows are indexed by the UTC day that *started*, so the adapter advances their labels by one day. Thus the signal includes 21 distinct consecutive event times through the cutoff. The latest close and daily marks use the daily fixing directly. Seven overlapping daily observations matched exactly as decimal strings with the one-day alignment. [PriceUSD field definition](https://coverage.coinmetrics.io/asset-metrics/PriceUSD)
- The paper fill is the `1m ReferenceRateUSD` at the exact UTC minute fixed by the registered decision and execution policy. The recorder must persist the decision before that event. A smoothing/aggregation interval can overlap the preceding minute(s). Receiving a fixing establishes its availability at the receipt time; it does not imply the fixing was known at its provider timestamp.
- The free Community reference series offers a short trailing window, documented as seven points. Failure to retrieve the exact event records a miss. The recorder cannot select a more attractive fixing or use a later quote to reconstruct a missed fill. Signal generation must enforce its decision window independently of the 48-hour daily mark recovery ceiling. Late marks retain their actual receipt times and recovery classification.
- A signal read uses two GETs. Each ordinary mark or paper-quote read uses one. The adapter caps each HTTP body at 24,576 bytes, with a 10-second socket timeout and zero retries. The calling job supplies the whole-job deadline. It accepts only the requested ordered dates, metric, finite positive prices and asset, rejecting duplicates, gaps, pagination and future timestamps.
- Each capture records the raw response SHA-256, exact request URL, response size, and UTC local receipt time after the response bytes arrive. Exact prices labeled by market event time are separated from receipt times. Read original captured values when replaying a decision. Provider corrections must be additive with their own hashes and actual later receipt times.

The rehearsal in `reports/prospective/source-rehearsal/` used an unscheduled date, recorded the signals before collecting the preselected later minute fixing, and included a separate delayed close read. It contributes zero prospective observations.

The seven-date field comparison is saved in `alignment.json`. The rehearsal checks the minute proxy's observed availability on that date. It does not establish a permanent midnight publication latency or scheduler punctuality. The prospective rules must record a miss when the required fixing is unavailable by its cutoff.
