# Start here

1. [Install the published toolkit](user-guide.md) and reproduce the four BTC studies from a writable folder.
2. Follow the findings from [daily momentum](../reports/btc-financial-v1/README.md) through [cost thresholds](../reports/btc-cost-break-even-v1/README.md) to [weekly decisions](../reports/btc-weekly-cadence-v1/README.md) and [weekly allocation](../reports/btc-weekly-allocation-v1/README.md). The [six-study programme](../research/programme.json) links each completed comparison to its protocol and source.
3. [Use the optional Codex skill](plugin-guide.md) from its own source checkout.
4. Explore [European options](options-conventions.md), [portfolio accounting](trading-conventions.md) or the separate [Q2 2025 historical example](historical-workflow.md).
5. Follow the [research](../research/README.md) and [experiment](../experiments/README.md) records into [Agenthon engineering decisions](agenthon-architecture.md) and [lessons from failed runs](negative-results.md).

For implementation, read the [development guide](development.md) and [financial workbench architecture](financial-workbench.md). For reproducibility, see the [methodology](methodology.md) and [v0.5.0 qualification](releases/v0.5.0.md).

The dated v0.1 audit documents and reports are historical receipts. Some retain
local paths into the owner's original archive, which is not bundled or required
for installation. The canonical public research/experiment JSON provides concise
summaries and source identities; it does not pretend private historical material
is publicly reproducible. See [public-content policy](publication.md).
