## Regression cases worth retaining

Quant Lab's own fixtures should demonstrate that nonfinite inputs cannot become a successful result; undefined Greeks are explicitly unavailable; independent properties detect perturbed option values; future observations cannot alter earlier decisions; and cash, trades, fees, positions and P&L reconcile. These are general mechanisms, not copies of official test cases. Passing these checks establishes the stated numerical/accounting scope, not predictive skill.

No promotion follows automatically from a passing synthetic fixture. Future real-model or strategy comparisons require separate evidence, fixed conditions and preservation of unsuccessful outcomes.
