## Evidence coverage and limits

The archive is curated, not exhaustive: 12 papers, 17 records, 27 hashed historical source references. Official-associated aggregates, local live-model results, synthetic controls, incomplete evaluation and NOT_RUN proposals remain separate. Missing billing, model revision, code or grading measurements stay unknown. Raw historical evidence may remain owner-held, so public independent reproduction is limited.

The Python engine and synthetic reports are reproducible from the recorded source/dependencies. Synthetic strategy losses establish no market edge. No new Agenthon evaluation, House/NVIDIA inference, Docker build, paid API/data/cloud service or financial transaction was performed; separately billed cost is $0. Broader financial features, forecasting and FFT methods remain proposed or deferred. Windows and additional Python versions remain unqualified.

Next milestone: owner review of the website PR, then broader installation coverage and additional evidence-backed archive entries.
