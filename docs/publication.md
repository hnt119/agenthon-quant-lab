# Public content and historical provenance

This repository contains maintained code, original synthetic fixtures, research
summaries, source identities and inspectable example results. The v0.3.1 distribution includes two separately attributed BTC data products. The Q2 2025 example uses CC BY 4.0. The 2017–2024 study extract and derived results use CC BY-SA 4.0. [Q2 data notice](../examples/data/bitstamp/README.md) · [Study attribution and changes](../experiments/btc-financial-v1/README.md#attribution-and-redistribution) It does not contain
organizer-only data/checkers, hidden references, canaries, private captures,
credentials, team claim proofs, submission ZIPs or copyrighted paper PDFs.

The original two commits and annotated v0.1.0 tag are preserved. Their historical
receipts contain nonsecret author metadata, local paths and source references.
Those records are not portable public-source links; a newer export cannot erase
old Git history, and no history rewrite was performed. Use the canonical research
and experiment indexes to browse curated public summaries. Raw historical evidence
may remain owner-held, which limits independent public reproduction.

Historical official scores retain their evidence provenance and are not freshly
verified platform claims, current rankings or toolkit accuracy metrics. Research
paper improvements are never relabeled as improvements measured on our agent.

The full baseline history was inspected before publication; the final release
adds a delta audit tied to its exact code version. Automated secret-pattern and
export checks complement that review; they do not prove exhaustive ownership or
that arbitrary future inputs are safe to publish.

User-created run bundles include input data and local provenance and are private
by default. The sanitized website projection is a separate allowlisted artifact.
See [attribution](attribution.md) for license boundaries.
