# What a result establishes

## Two kinds of reproducibility

A **new deterministic run** stores its explicit specification, byte snapshots,
input hashes, source fingerprint/commit, dependency versions, results, checks and
report. Exact reproduction uses the same source and inputs; an assumption change
creates a derived run. Package versions are pinned for the verified environment.
Hashes identify bytes, not authorship or a cryptographic attestation.

A **historical Agenthon observation** is a summary tied to immutable receipt paths,
commits and hashes. Some raw artifacts are owner-held and unavailable to public
readers. Historical inference is not rerun during installation or archive search;
billing, served model revision and missing outputs stay unknown where unrecorded.

## Evidence categories

Official-reported aggregates, local live-model tests, synthetic infrastructure
checks, incomplete runs and NOT_RUN proposals are distinct. Unknown is not zero;
not executed is not failed. Results on selected tasks retain the selected and full
roster denominators. No benchmark pass rate is presented as trading profitability.

A paper's reported result belongs to its evaluated models, data and protocol.
`adaptation_status` states our relationship; linked experiments provide context,
not evidence that we implemented or reproduced the entire paper method.

## Numerical and accounting verification

Option checks combine bounds, parity, independent discounted-payoff quadrature,
and two finite-difference scales. Trading checks independently reconcile the ledger
and test signal timing. Tolerances and unavailable boundary checks are explicit;
see the two convention guides for exact definitions. The browser demo is a smaller
zero-dividend price calculator checked against 64 Python references within its
declared approximation error. It is not a second full finance engine.

Examples are authored synthetic fixtures. We preserve negative outcomes and never
tune the strategy on the final held-out period. Validating arithmetic does not
validate data provenance, model assumptions, a predictive edge or execution in a
real market. A future research claim needs representative licensed data, a fixed
protocol and independent evaluation.
